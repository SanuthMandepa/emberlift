Steel, chalk
and a running clock

A training log is an instrument, not a magazine. These ten screens treat it that way: a dark panel, tabular numerals you can read at arm's length, and a single volt accent that is spent only on two things, what is live right now and what you just beat. Every screen names the requirements it satisfies, so the design can be reviewed against the spec rather than against taste.

Colour discipline

  • Volt means live or achieved. Nothing else.
  • Flare means a record, or an area falling behind.
  • Ice means time: rest, recovery, clocks.
  • State never rides on colour alone. Every one carries an icon or a word.

Gym floor rules

  • Log a set in one tap, thumb reach, 48px minimum.
  • Numerals are tabular so weights line up down a column.
  • Nothing on the session screen waits on the network.
  • Readable at arm's length under bad gym lighting.
